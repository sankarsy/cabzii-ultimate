# SEO content and backlink plan — Cabzii

Ethical growth plan only. **Do not** buy spam links, use link farms, PBNs, automated blast tools, comment spam, or fake reviews.

This plan supports crawlability and relevance. It does **not** guarantee rankings or lead volume.

---

## High-quality backlink strategy

1. **Earn links with useful guides** — airport pickup timelines, outstation fare explainers, city travel tips that cite Cabzii pages naturally.
2. **Partner exchanges** — hotels, resorts, event planners, corporate travel desks linking to real booking pages.
3. **Local citations** — consistent NAP for Chennai HQ across directories (name, address, phone, website).
4. **Digital PR** — festival travel advisories, monsoon driving tips, Tirupati / hill-station season guides pitched to local publishers.
5. **Resource pages** — ask tourism boards / chambers for inclusion only when Cabzii genuinely serves that market.

---

## Business & travel directories

Submit accurate listings (one profile per platform):

- Google Business Profile (HQ + service areas as appropriate)
- Bing Places
- Justdial / IndiaMART / Sulekha (where relevant)
- Tourism / travel association directories in Tamil Nadu and South India
- Airport transfer / chauffeur directories that require verification

Keep category, phone, and website identical.

---

## Local business listings

- Claim and complete Google Business Profile
- Add real photos of vehicles/office (when available)
- Respond to real customer reviews only
- Use service-area business settings honestly

---

## Partner websites

Priority partners:

- Hotels near major airports (MAA, BLR, HYD, CJB)
- Wedding venues needing transport packages
- Corporate offices with shuttle / outstation needs
- Package tour operators needing last-mile cabs

Offer a clear partner landing page (no doorway spam).

---

## Hotel and tourism partnerships

- Reciprocal “airport transfer” and “local cab” links on hotel “Getting there” pages
- Cabzii hotel pages (when public) link back to airport taxi + local cabs + holiday packages
- Co-branded itineraries only when both parties deliver the service

---

## Travel blogs & guest posts

Pitch topics with original data or checklists:

- “How to book airport taxi from Chennai Airport”
- “Acting driver vs full cab hire — when each makes sense”
- “One-way outstation cab tips for Bengaluru–Chennai”
- “Group bus rental checklist for weddings”

Require editorial quality; decline paid spam placements.

---

## Useful travel guides (on-site content)

Publish on `/blogs` and link to matching service/city hubs:

| Theme | Example angles | Link to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cab travel | Fare components, vehicle types, night charges | `/cabs`, city hubs |
| Airport | Terminal pickup points, waiting rules | `/services/airport-taxi/{city}` |
| City | Local packages, popular pickups | `/cab-booking/{city}` |
| Route | Distance, typical duration, stops | `/routes/...` |
| Holiday | Destination seasonality | `/holidays`, package landings |
| Hotel | Getting there by cab | airport + local cab pages |
| Acting driver | Hourly vs daily, own-car requirements | `/acting-driver/{city}` |
| Bus rental | Seating, permits, group tips | `/buses`, bus rental city pages |

Each guide needs unique photos, city-specific facts, and a clear CTA (Book Now / Get Fare / WhatsApp).

## What not to do

- Buying bulk backlinks or “SEO packages” of spam domains
- Link farms / PBNs / automated outreach blasts
- Fake reviews, fake ratings, fake bookings
- Comment spam or scraped doorway city pages
- Keyword stuffing or hidden text
